Excited to share my latest project: BorrowLoop — Borrow Instead of Buy. 🔁

We all have expensive tools and gear sitting idle at home. BorrowLoop connects neighbors to borrow rarely used items (power tools, tripods, project gear) safely and locally:

• No catalog browsing: State what you need, get matched with nearby neighbors within your pincode.

• Built-in privacy: Contact info and exact house numbers stay locked until a pickup request is confirmed.

• Zero lender risk: Full security deposits are held during the loan and returned once the item is checked back in.

Available on both web and Android.
